# AI disclosure

Hoard was made with a lot of help from AI.

**Who did what:** Claude, an AI assistant made by Anthropic, wrote most of the code, the visual design and
logo, and the documentation, including this page, working from the maintainer's direction. The maintainer,
Soloflighter1010, decided what Hoard should do, tried it against real store accounts, reported what went
wrong, and publishes the releases.

**What that means for you:**

- The tools have been tested, both against stand-ins for each store and with real accounts. Even so,
  AI-written code can contain mistakes, including security mistakes. If you find one, please report it
  as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) describes.
- Hoard doesn't use AI while it runs, and nothing about you or your library is ever sent to an AI
  service. AI was only used to build it.
- The privacy policy, terms of use and other documents were drafted with AI help and haven't been
  reviewed by a lawyer.
- Hoard isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic.
