A bookmark for products and sets

Send to Hoard

On a product's page on Booth, Gumroad, Jinxxy, Payhip or itch.io, this bookmark sends it to Hoard, to add to a wishlist. On a set someone shared from Hoard, it sends you the set.

Send to Hoard

Drag it to your browser's bookmarks bar. It needs Hoard 5.2.0 beta 11 or newer, which opens hoard:// links.

Using it

  1. Add it to your bookmarks bar.

    Drag Send to Hoard, above, to the bar. Don't see the bar? Press Ctrl+Shift+B (Cmd+Shift+B on a Mac) to show it. Or right-click the button, copy its address (Copy link address, or Copy Link in Firefox and Safari), make a new bookmark and paste it as the bookmark's address.

  2. Open a product's page.

    Any product on Booth, Gumroad, Jinxxy, Payhip or itch.io, or a set someone shared from Hoard (its hoard.furryup.link/set.html#… link).

  3. Choose the bookmark.

    Your browser asks whether to open Hoard. Hoard comes to the front and asks which wishlist the product goes in (or a new one), with its link already in Add products by link. For a set, Add a shared set opens with the set in it.

  4. Say so in Hoard.

    Nothing is added until you choose Add them (or add the set) there.

What it does, and doesn't

  • It runs only when you choose it, on the page you're on. Anywhere but a product's page or a shared set, it just says what it works on.
  • It sends only the page's address, to Hoard on your own computer, as a hoard:// link. Nothing from the page itself, no cookies, and nothing to any server.
  • Hoard checks the link again, reads the product's public page as anyone can see it, and adds nothing until you say so.
  • It's the same bookmark Hoard offers under a wishlist's Find more to add (where copy it copies it too), and its code is in Hoard's hoard/web/shared.js, where it's called SEND_TO_HOARD.

Why a page of its own? Hoard opens in a window of its own, and a link can't be dragged from it into your browser's bookmarks bar. More on hoard:// links in Hoard: Sets and Wishlists.
