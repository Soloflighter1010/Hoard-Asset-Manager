Questions

Before you grab it

Does Hoard need my store passwords?
No. You sign in to each store once, on that store's own page in a browser window Hoard opens for it. Hoard keeps the resulting sign-in encrypted in your computer's keyring and never sees your password.
Does it download from every store?
Booth, Gumroad, Jinxxy and itch.io, yes, into Store/Creator/Product folders. Payhip is listed, not downloaded: Hoard shows everything you bought there with each product's download page, and you fetch the files from Payhip yourself.
Will it replace the Creator Companion or ALCOM?
It works alongside them. Hoard shows the same project list, can make a new project from VRChat's templates with products from your library already in, updates packages, backs projects up and keeps their history with Git.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Your library, tags and downloaded files are on your computer. You only need a connection to sync with the stores.
Isn't Hoard open source?
It is, and it stays that way. Getting it here supports its development and puts it in your Gumroad library. The source and every release are on GitHub.
