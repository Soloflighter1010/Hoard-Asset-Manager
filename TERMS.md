## 5. Short links and Hoard's share server

**Make a short link**, in a set's **Share...**, keeps a locked copy of the set on Hoard's share server, which the
maintainer runs on Cloudflare. It's optional: a set's long link never uses the share server. What the server keeps,
and for how long, is in [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).

- **Only for sets.** Use the share server only for short links to sets, made by the app. Don't use it to keep or

- **What you share is yours to answer for.** You're responsible for what a set you share holds, short link or long:
  its name, your note and your photo links. Don't share anything unlawful, or anything you don't have the right to
  share.
- **No promise it's there.** The share server is free and comes with no guarantee. A short link lasts 14 days from
  when it's made or last updated, and can stop working sooner: when its sharer deletes it, when the server has
  reached its daily limits or can't be reached, or if the service changes or ends. Keep the long link of anything
  that matters.
- **Reported links.** The maintainer can't see what's in a short link, but may delete one that's reported for
  abuse, or that breaks these terms, without notice. To report one, open an issue on GitHub with its id (the 10
  letters and digits after `S1.`), not the whole link: the whole link opens the set for anyone who reads it.
