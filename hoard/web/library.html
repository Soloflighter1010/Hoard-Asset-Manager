        Stores

        Sign in to each store once. Refresh reads what you've bought; nothing is downloaded.

        Your sign-ins stay on this computer, a separate one for each store, encrypted by your operating system, in a folder only Hoard uses. Hoard never keeps your store passwords. Never share that folder. Store sign-ins aren't saved on this computer, because it has no keyring to protect them. Install and unlock one (GNOME Keyring, KeePassXC or KWallet), then sign in again.

        If a store blocks the automatic refresh, open your library there in your usual browser, scroll to the bottom, press Ctrl+S and save it as "Webpage, Single File". Then choose Import page, or drop the file on this window.

        Drop a saved store page to add it to your hoard
        You're offline. Your library, search and saved images still work; refreshing and store links need a connection.
