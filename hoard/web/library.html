        Stores

        Sign in to each store once. Refresh reads what you've bought; nothing is downloaded.

        Quick refresh looks for what's new: it reads only the start of each library and of the wishlists you keep, the first 2 pages or the first 50 products, whichever is fewer. What it doesn't reach stays as it was; Refresh reads everything.

        Your sign-ins stay on this computer, a separate one for each store, encrypted by your operating system, in a folder only Hoard uses. Hoard never keeps your store passwords. Never share that folder. Store sign-ins aren't saved on this computer, because it has no keyring to protect them. Install and unlock one (GNOME Keyring, KeePassXC or KWallet), then sign in again.

        Import saved pages

        Instead of signing in, or when a store blocks the refresh: open your library in your usual browser, scroll to the bottom, press Ctrl+S and save it as "Webpage, Single File". Save each page of it (for Payhip, the bookmark below saves a whole shop at once), then import them all together, or drop them, or their folder, on this window.

        Payhip: every page of a shop in one go. Add the Save for Hoard bookmark to your usual browser: get it from its page, where you drag it to the bookmarks bar, or and make a bookmark with it as the address. Then open a shop's library page (its address followed by /b-account) and choose the bookmark: it saves all that shop's pages as one file. Do the same on each shop, and import the files together.

        Drop saved store pages, or a folder of them, to add them to your hoard
        You're offline. Your library, search and saved images still work; refreshing and store links need a connection.

          Settings

          Changes are saved as you make them.

          Downloads folder

          Where downloaded files are saved. Changing it doesn't move files you've already downloaded.

          Library folders

          Your downloads can live on more than one drive. Add a folder on another drive (an external disk, say) and Hoard reads it as part of your library, with a tab of its own in Downloads.

          New downloads go to the downloads folder; a product already in another folder is kept up to date there. To move products between folders, open one in Downloads, or select several. Removing a folder only stops Hoard reading it: its files stay where they are.

          Hoard's folders

          Stores

          Payhip shops

          Payhip keeps your purchases in each shop you bought from, not in one library. Add each shop's address (it's in your purchase email), one per line. A shop at payhip.com/… usually lists what you bought from every shop, but Hoard only opens the links of the shops listed here. Importing a shop's saved page adds it for you. Hoard lists what you bought on Payhip; you download it from Payhip yourself.

          Store tags

          On Booth, Jinxxy and itch.io: a few products' public pages each time a store is read, until each one has been read once. Their tags show as suggestions on them, and you can filter by them; they never change yours.

          Offline

          Files to leave out

          Every download leaves these out, from every store or one: Sync, Download new, and a product's own Download. When a product downloads, Hoard asks which of its files to save (above): what you leave out of it, it remembers, and asks again next time.

            New in your library

            Things that appear in your library after a refresh get a New badge for this long. Recently added (in Sort) lists the newest first.

            Failed downloads

            A download that fails (a dropped connection, a busy server) is tried again after a short wait before it counts as failed.

            Previous versions

            When an update replaces a file, the old one is kept in _Previous versions in the product's folder, and Restore in Downloads puts it back.

            Routine check

            While Hoard is open, it does this often: reads your stores (not Payhip, which needs you there for its bot check), checks your downloads are as it downloaded them, and checks for updates. Then it asks you which of what it found to download: nothing downloads without you. Anything you start goes first.

            Notifications through Apprise

            Send the news you choose to Discord, Telegram, ntfy, email and many more, through Apprise: an address for each. Each holds its service's token, so Hoard keeps them as it keeps API keys, never in config.json, and shows each one only by its service and what tells it apart, never its token. What's sent says how many, never the name of a product in your library; on Discord it's a rich message, in Hoard's colour.

              Accessibility

              Motion is also reduced when your computer's settings ask for less of it.

              Browser for store sign-ins

              It can be any browser built on Chromium: Edge, Chrome, Brave, Vivaldi, Opera, or another you choose by its program (Arc, Thorium...); not Firefox or Safari. It keeps its own sign-ins, apart from the browser you browse with. A store you've signed in to keeps the browser it was signed in with until you sign in to it again.

              Closing Hoard

              Downloads, syncs and automatic syncs carry on with the window closed. Open Hoard again (from the Start menu) to bring the window back, and quit it here, with Quit Hoard.

              Hoard updates

              Each time Hoard starts, and once a day while it's open, it asks GitHub (where Hoard is published) whether there's a newer version, in the background. GitHub sees your IP address, as with any website; nothing else is sent.

              Betas are the next version before it's finished, for testers: they may have problems. With this on, a beta counts as an update too, and you're offered the finished version when it's out.

              What's new

              Backup

              Your settings, tags, sets, choices and library list in one file, in Documents › Hoard backups: to keep, or to move to another computer. Never your sign-ins, keys or downloads.

              Bring in from another tool

              Coming from KonoAsset? Hoard can bring in its library: what you own is matched by its BOOTH item, and the rest come in as Local products, left where they are, with their tags and memos. Nothing in KonoAsset's folder is changed.

              Troubleshooting

              Something isn't working? Create a local, anonymized support report. Nothing is uploaded automatically, and the report can be reviewed before you share it.
