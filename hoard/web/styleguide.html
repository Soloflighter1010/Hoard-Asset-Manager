Hoard style guide

Every part of Hoard's pages, drawn with the page's own stylesheet, the styles and the theme chosen in the bar above, as the app serves them, so this page is always as the app is. Copy an example's markup (under Markup) and it looks the same on the page. Use the colours by name (var(--gold)), never as values: every theme, Light and Dark, and the colour-blind store colours change them. The Treasure and Workbench styles (web/looks.css) restyle the same markup: a part needs nothing more to work in all three. Everything here is made up.

Colours

A theme is these colours, dark and light (hoard/themes.py). Each theme keeps to the same promises: text 4.5:1 on its backgrounds, the accent as text 4.5:1, text on the accent 4.5:1, and each store's colour 3:1 on the page.

Stores

Each store has its colour, used for its dot, a tile's spine (its notch) and its tab. The class is the store's own name in small letters: booth, gumroad, jinxxy, payhip, itch, local.

Sets and Wishlists isn't a store: its tab has a colour of its own (--sets, set on [data-store="Sets"]) and a heart for its dot (dot sets).

Buttons

Gold (primary) is the one thing to do; plain (ghost) is the rest. sm is the size beside fields and in panels. A danger button says what it does ("Delete"), never just "OK".

Fields

A dropdown is a plain select: looks.css draws every one in the theme's colours with Sort's arrow, and its open list too where the browser allows it. Its own rules give it a size and corners, never another ground or arrow.

Chips and tags

Filters are chips: pressed is on (aria-pressed="true"). A tag on a product is a pill with its own remove button; a suggested tag is a chip with add.

Tiles

A product is a tile: its picture (or a lettered stand-in, ph), its store's spine (notch, striped with another store's colour when you own it there too), and its name and creator. Workbench shows the same tiles as rows, from row-meta; Treasure frames each picture in its store's colour, named from the notch's data-s.

Details

A product's details: its picture, name, creator and store, one gold button for the thing to do, the store's own pages beside it, then its marks, tags and sets. Treasure adds the row of facts (d-stats).

Projects

Projects is projects, the whole window below the bar: its fields, buttons and lists take their look from inside it. Its views are tabs (pj-tabs), as are a project's (pj-tabs pj-subs); what its own lane of tasks is doing shows above them (pj-job). Its rows alternate, and the one under the pointer is outlined.
