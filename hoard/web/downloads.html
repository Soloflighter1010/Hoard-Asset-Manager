          Settings

          Changes are saved as you make them.

          Downloads folder

          Where downloaded files are saved. Changing it doesn't move files you've already downloaded.

          Other library folders

          Folders on other drives that Hoard reads as part of your library, such as an external drive. New downloads go to the downloads folder; a product already in one of these is kept up to date where it is. To move a product to another of your folders, open its details. Removing a folder here only stops Hoard reading it: its files stay where they are.

          Stores

          Payhip shops

          Payhip keeps your purchases in each shop you bought from, not in one library. Add each shop's address (it's in your purchase email), one per line. Importing a shop's saved page adds it for you. Hoard lists what you bought on Payhip; you download it from Payhip yourself.

          Offline

          New in your library

          Things that appear in your library after a refresh get a New badge for this long. Recently added (in Sort) lists the newest first.

          Failed downloads

          A download that fails (a dropped connection, a busy server) is tried again after a short wait before it counts as failed.

          Check your downloads

          While Hoard is open, it checks this often that your downloads are as it downloaded them: none missing, and none changed since. A file is read in full only the first time, or after it changed. Downloads shows when it last checked; Tasks says which files, if any.

          Sync automatically

          While Hoard is open, it syncs by itself this often: it reads your stores and downloads anything new or updated, counting from your last sync. Payhip is left out, since it needs you there for its bot check.

          Accessibility

          Motion is also reduced when your computer's settings ask for less of it.

          Browser for store sign-ins

          After changing the browser, you may need to sign in to your stores again.

          Closing Hoard

          Downloads, syncs and automatic syncs carry on with the window closed. Open Hoard again (from the Start menu) to bring the window back, and quit it here, with Quit Hoard.

          Updates

          Once a day, when Hoard starts, it asks GitHub (where Hoard is published) whether there's a newer version. GitHub sees your IP address, as with any website; nothing else is sent.

          Betas are the next version before it's finished, for testers: they may have problems. With this on, a beta counts as an update too, and you're offered the finished version when it's out.
