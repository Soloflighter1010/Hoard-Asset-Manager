Hoard isn't the only tool VRChat creators use. Three others come up most: VRChat's own **Creator Companion**
(VCC), **ALCOM** and **KonoAsset**. They each do a different job, and many people use more than one:

- **The Creator Companion** is VRChat's official tool for Unity projects and their packages (the VRChat SDK, and
  packages from other people's repositories).
- **ALCOM** is a fast, open-source alternative to the Creator Companion, for Windows, macOS and Linux.
- **KonoAsset** organises the VRChat assets you've already downloaded, by type, category, tag and the avatar they
  fit.
- **Hoard** reads what you've bought from the stores, downloads it and keeps it up to date, and manages your Unity
  projects too.

They work together. Hoard's Projects keeps the same list as the Creator Companion and ALCOM, so a project made in one
shows in the others.

## Side by side

What each one does, from its own documentation, as of November 2026. Tools change, so check their pages, linked at
the bottom.

| | Hoard | Creator Companion | ALCOM | KonoAsset |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **What it's for** | What you've bought: listed, downloaded, kept up to date, and put into projects | VRChat's Unity projects and their packages | The Creator Companion's job, faster, on more systems | Organising the assets you've downloaded |
| **Your stores** | Reads what you own on Booth, Gumroad, Jinxxy, Payhip and itch.io: signed in as you, or from pages you saved | No | No | Fills in a product's details from its BOOTH link |
| **Downloads and updates** | Downloads what's new and what's changed, keeping the version before (Payhip's are listed, and you download them there) | Packages from VPM repositories | Packages from VPM repositories, several at once, with their changelogs | No: you add what you've downloaded (drag and drop; a .zip is unpacked) |
| **Organising** | Tags (yours and the creators'), kinds, sets you can share, wishlists with their prices, a search with prefixes | No | No | Types, categories, tags, notes and dependencies; search by the avatar an asset fits |
| **Unity projects** | Make one (VRChat's templates, or blank) with products from your library; packages and repositories; back up; Git source control | Make one from a template, add your own, migrate, back up; packages; other people's repositories (from 2.1.0) | Make one from a template (and your own templates, from 1.1.0), back up, change its Unity version, migrate; packages and repositories | No |
| **In Unity** | Hoard for Unity: your library in a window, importing products and sets, and their credits | The VRChat SDK | No | No |
| **Systems** | Windows, macOS and Linux | Windows 10 and 11 (on macOS and Linux, some of it from the command line) | Windows, macOS and Linux (Linux supported by its community) | Windows 10 and 11 |

## Which to use

- **Just making avatars or worlds, with VRChat's packages?** The Creator Companion is VRChat's own tool, and its
  documentation is where VRChat explains how to start.
- **The same, but faster, or on a Mac or Linux?** ALCOM does the Creator Companion's job with the same settings, so
  you can switch back and forth.
- **Want to sort what you've downloaded by hand, with notes and the avatars it fits?** KonoAsset is made for that.
- **Switching from KonoAsset to Hoard?** **Bring in from KonoAsset...**, in Hoard's Menu or in Settings, brings in its library
  (5.2.0 beta 5): matched to your Hoard library by the BOOTH item, the rest as Local products left where they are,
  with their tags and memos. See [Your Own Packages](Your-Own-Packages#from-konoasset).
- **Bought from several stores, and want it all in one place, downloaded and kept up to date?** That's Hoard. It
  puts your products into projects, and its Projects shares its list with the Creator Companion and ALCOM.
