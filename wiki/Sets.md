# Sets

A set groups products you use together: an avatar, its outfits and hair, and the shaders and tools they need.
From 4.2, a set can hold anything in your library, downloaded or not, and products you don't own yet: a set
someone shared with you lists what it needs, and where to buy each.

## The Sets tab

**Sets and Wishlists** (called **Sets** before 5.1) is a tab beside the stores, in the [Library](Library) and in
[Downloads](Downloads), with a colour and a heart of its own (and **Sets and Wishlists** in the bar at the top of every
page opens it in the Library). Each set is a big square tile: the picture you chose for it (see
[Its picture](#its-picture)), or four of its products' pictures, with how many products it has and how many are
downloaded, yours to download, or still to buy.

Sets are chosen as products are (**Select**, or Ctrl- or Shift-click). Right-click a set, or use the selection bar,
for **Open**, **Share...**, **Download the N not downloaded**, **New project with this set...** (5.2.0: Projects'
**New project**, with what's downloaded of the set already in it), **Choose a picture...**, **Rename** and **Delete**;
with several chosen, **Download** and **Delete** work on them all.

Open a set and it's a folder of its products. **Sets** above its name goes back to every set.

- **The ones on this page** are their usual tiles: open one for its details, or select several and use the
  selection bar or right-click as anywhere (**Sets**, **Take out of** this set, takes them out). In a set, these
  are all of its products here, whichever view (Library, Archive...) they're in.
- **The rest** come after them, each with its picture and where it is. In the Library: **Not yours yet**, with
  **Buy on** its store (opens the product's page in your browser), and **Your own packages (Local)**. In Downloads,
  also **In your library, not downloaded**, which opens it in the Library. **Remove** takes one out of the set (or off the wishlist), once you've said so
  (5.2.0).

A set also has **Share...**, **Download the N not downloaded** (every product of it that's yours and not downloaded,
at once, as **Download** does in the Library), **New project with this set...**, **Choose a picture...**, **Rename** and **Delete** (deleting a set
leaves its products as they are), and under **Note and photos** (folded away while there's nothing in them) a **Note** (what it's for, or
how to put it together) and **Photos** (below). Once they're saved, they show as they're shared, with **Edit** to
change them: it opens the note's box and the photos' **×**, and nothing is sent until **Save** (**Cancel** leaves
them as they were) (5.2.0 beta 2).

From 5.2.0 beta 4 an open set or wishlist reads like a product's details: its picture large beside its name, what's in
it and where it's from, **Share...** on its own, and the rest of what's done with it in a quieter row under it. Its
**Note and photos** are a card of their own, and a wishlist's **Find more to add** and price range are together in
another, just above its products.

## Making a set

- **New set**, in the Sets tab, then add products to it.
- **Drag and drop** (5.2.0 beta 4): drop a product on another to make a set of them, named as it's made (the
  products chosen with it go too), or on **Sets and Wishlists** (the tab above the tiles, or in the bar) to add it
  to a set you choose. What a drop will do shows on what's under the pointer as you drag; Escape cancels.
- Choose products in the **Library** or in **Downloads** (tick their boxes, Ctrl- or Shift-click, or **Select**
  on a touch screen), then **Sets** in the selection bar (or right-click): **New set from these...**, or **Add to**
  a set you have: the five you added to last (or the newest), and **Add to another set...** for the rest (5.2.0):
  every set's tile to choose from, with a box to find one by its name, then a question before anything's added (5.2.0
  beta 2). In the Library that works whether they're downloaded or not.
- A product's details, in the Library or in Downloads, list its sets: **Add** puts it in another, **×** takes it
  out, and a set's name opens it in the Sets tab.
- **Make a set** in **Projects** makes one of the products a Unity project uses.
- **Add products by link...**, in an open wishlist (from 5.1; from 5.2.0 on wishlists only, as a set is made from
  what's in your library): paste products' links on Booth, Gumroad,
  Jinxxy, Payhip or itch.io, one a line. Hoard reads each one's page as anyone sees it (no sign-in is sent), from the
  store's own site, for its name, creator, price and picture, and adds it, owned or not. A link that isn't a
  product's page, or whose page can't be read, is left out, and Hoard says why.
- **Find more to add**, on an open wishlist (5.2.0): type what you're after, then choose **Nexyy** (a search
  across the stores) or **Booth**, **Gumroad**, **Jinxxy** or **itch.io**: the search opens in your browser. Copy
  the link of a product you want there and add it with **Add products by link...** (a link from Nexyy works too).
  Hoard doesn't read these searches itself.

A set remembers what each product is when it's added (its name, creator, store page and picture), so it can be
shown and shared whether or not the product is in your library now. Products are known by the same key tags use
(their store and name, without versions or labels), so a product stays in its set when it's updated or moved to
another library folder.

## Mix and match

**Mix and match...**, at the top of the Sets tab, picks an outfit at random from what you own (5.0): an avatar, and
one product from each of your Clothing tags (tops, shoes, hair...), for something new from what you have. It takes
them from your [kinds of tags](Tags#kinds-of-tags): avatars are what has a tag whose kind is **Avatar**, and clothes
are what has a tag whose kind is **Clothing**. Until you have both, it says how to set them up.

- **Keep** beside a pick keeps it; **Pick again** picks the rest again.
- **Only clothes that share a tag with the avatar** (on to begin with) picks clothes that have one of the avatar's
  other tags too, such as its name, so they're made for it. When nothing of a Clothing tag does, that pick is from
  all of them, and Hoard says which.
- **Make a set of them** makes a new set of the avatar and its outfit.

## Sharing a set

**Share...** shares a set as a short link to the set viewer on Hoard's website, such as
`https://hoard.furryup.link/set.html#S1.Xq3vT9bKa2.Rk2Lw0pY7cHq-4nZsV1aEg`, about 70 characters, that works for 14
days: **Make a short link**, then **Copy the short link** or **Open it**. From 5.0 a short link is the only way to
share a set: the long link earlier versions made held the whole set, and was too long to pass around. Anyone who
opens the link sees the set's name, your note and photos, and each product with its picture and a link to its store
page. Pasting the link into **Add a shared set...** in their Sets tab adds the set to their Hoard: what they own
already shows as theirs, and what they don't says where to buy it. Sets added from a link show apart from your own,
under **Sets you added from a link** (5.2.0 beta 2; ones added before then stay with yours).

- **What's in it:** the set's name, note and photo links, and each product's store, name, creator, public store
  page and picture address. Not your library, your downloads, your tags or anything else of yours.
- **Only public pages:** a product's store page in a shared set is its public page on the store (or, where Hoard
  doesn't know it, its creator's page), never one of yours, such as a Gumroad purchase's download page or your
  Jinxxy inventory. Gumroad and Jinxxy products get their own page from your next refresh of that store. (Hoard
  4.2.0 put your Gumroad download pages in shared links: the viewer no longer shows them, but if you shared a set
  with Gumroad products from 4.2.0, delete those posts and share it again.)
- **What's never in one:** your own products (**Local**) and hidden products, whether the hidden library is
  locked or not. Sharing says how many were left out.
- **Pictures:** each product's picture loads from its store's own image host (Booth's, Gumroad's, Jinxxy's and so
  on), so Hoard hosts nothing; one without a picture shows its initials, as in Hoard. Store pages are only links to
  the stores' own websites.
- **A long link from before 5.0** (`set.html#H1.…`, with the set itself after the `#`) still opens in the viewer,
  and in **Add a shared set...**, as does its code.
- **An Adult set** (5.2): a set with a product for adults in it (as its store, its tags or you say: see
  [Library](Library#products-for-adults)) is marked 18+, on its tile and its page, until the last of them is taken
  out; it can't be taken off by hand before then. Its viewer is asked whether they're 18 or older before anything
  is shown. Under 18, they see the set without its products for adults and without your photos. The answer is kept
  in a cookie in their own browser, for that page alone: 18 or older for a year, so they aren't asked each time,
  and under 18 until they close the browser, so they're asked again next time. A set shown without its
  products for adults says so in a box at the top, and **Change your answer** forgets the answer. Hoard never asks anyone for ID, and the answer is sent nowhere: see
  [Security and privacy](Security-and-Privacy).

### Short links

- **What's uploaded:** a locked copy of the set, kept on Hoard's share server (`hoard-share.furryup.link`) for 14
  days. Hoard locks it on your computer first (AES-GCM, with a new random key), and the key is only in the link,
  after the `#`, which a browser never sends anywhere. So the share server can't read the set, and neither can
  anyone who runs it: only someone with the link can.
- **Yours to keep going, or to change:** Hoard keeps a private token for each short link, which only it has.
  **Update the link** puts the set as it is now behind the same link and keeps it 14 days from today, so update
  it before it runs out to share the set for longer: Hoard doesn't do it by itself. The dialog says when the set
  has changed since. **Delete the short link** deletes it from the server at once, and deleting the set deletes
  its short link too. Whoever opens a deleted or run-out link is told it's gone.
- **How long it still works** (5.2): a set with a short link says on its tile how many days are left (in gold for
  the last two), and on its page until when. Once it has run out, it says so and when, until **Share...** makes a
  new one.
- **Opening one:** the viewer fetches the locked set from the share server by the link's id, and unlocks it in
  the page. **Add a shared set...** does the same.
- **Limits:** a short link holds a set of up to about 500 products (more or fewer, as their names and links are
  short or long). The share server can make a limited number a day: if it can't, or can't be reached, Hoard says
  so, and you can try again later.
- **Too big for one link** (a long wishlist, say): **Share...** says so, and offers **Split into N sets**. That
  makes new sets of its products, in its order, each as full as one short link holds: "Booth: Loved items (1 of
  2)", "(2 of 2)", and so on, each with the set's note and photos. Share each one. The set itself stays as it
  is, so a wishlist read from a store is still brought up to date when you read it again; its parts are
  wishlists of your own, as they were when you split it.

## Its picture

**Choose a picture...** in a set takes a picture from your computer for its tile: a PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF, made at
most 640 pixels across. Hoard keeps it in `set-pictures` in its app-data folder; it isn't shared with the set (a
link holds no pictures) or kept in backups. **Take the picture off** goes back to its products' pictures. Your own
packages can have a picture of your choosing too: see [Your own packages](Your-Own-Packages#its-picture).

## Photos

A set can have up to 8 of your own photos of it (an avatar wearing the outfit, say), shown in the viewer with the
set. Hoard doesn't host pictures: put them on a photo site, then **Add a photo link...** with the link to the
picture, or to its page there, as the site's Share or Copy link gives it (`https://imgur.com/fOJAk3L`, a Bluesky
post, a Gyazo or ImgBB page): Hoard reads that page once for the picture it shows, and keeps the picture's own link.
Pictures from these sites are accepted: Imgur (`i.imgur.com`), Gyazo (`i.gyazo.com`), X (`pbs.twimg.com`),
Bluesky (`cdn.bsky.app`), Reddit (`i.redd.it`), Postimages (`i.postimg.cc`), ImgBB (`i.ibb.co`), Discord
(`cdn.discordapp.com`, `media.discordapp.net`), and the stores' own image hosts. The viewer shows pictures only
from those sites. Where a page's picture can't be found (an album, or a post with several), open the picture
itself and copy its own link (right-click it, **Copy image address**). Discord's links stop working after a while,
so for a set you'll share for long, use another site.

### What's in a photo, marked

Once a set's note and photos are saved (5.2), its page shows each photo, with a numbered mark where each product
marked on it is, and the same numbers listed beside it with each product's picture, name and store. Point at a mark
(or move to it with the keyboard) to see its product; choose it to open the product's details, or its store page
when it isn't on the page. The viewer shows them the same way to whoever opens the set. A set you added from
someone else's link shows its photos once you choose **Show the photos**: they're on sites that person chose, and
Hoard asks those sites for them only when you say so. While your hidden products are locked, saving what's marked
on a photo keeps their marks as they were.

**Mark products on it...** (or **Change what's marked...**) opens the photo with the set's products beside it.
Choose a product's **Place**, then click where it is on the photo; **Move** puts it somewhere else, and **Take off**
takes it off. By keyboard, **Place** puts the mark in the middle of the photo, the arrow keys move it (with Shift,
in bigger steps) and Delete takes it off. Nothing's kept until **Save**.

- A photo can have up to 30 products marked on it, each once.
- Each mark is kept as a share of the photo's width and height, so the marks line up at whatever size the photo is
  shown. A product taken out of the set, or a photo taken off, takes its marks with it.
- Hoard shows the photo by fetching it from its photo site once, and keeping it, as it does for product pictures.
- The marks are shared with the set, locked inside its short link with the rest. Your own products (**Local**)
  can be marked, but aren't shared, so their marks aren't either.

## In Hoard for Unity

Hoard for Unity's **Sets** pill lists your sets, and **Import set** imports the downloaded products in one go
(see [Hoard for Unity](Hoard-for-Unity)). Products a set has that aren't downloaded aren't in Unity yet:
**Download the N not downloaded** in the Sets tab brings them in, and buying the ones that aren't yours.

## Wishlists

From 4.4, the Sets tab has your **Wishlists** too, below your sets: what you'd like from each store, with what each
product costs. A wishlist is a set (you can share it, give it a picture, a note and photos), and the products
already in your library show as yours.

- **Read my wishlists...** reads them from the stores you choose, each in its own signed-in window, as a refresh
  reads your library: Booth's loved items (all of them as one wishlist, and each list of them you made as one more),
  each of your Gumroad wishlists, your Jinxxy wishlist, likes and each of your lists, and the wishlists in each Payhip
  shop you've added. It runs as a task, in **Tasks**.
- **Import saved wishlist pages...** reads wishlist pages you saved from your own browser instead (press Ctrl+S and
  save as "Webpage, Single File"), on your computer, with nothing sent anywhere. A long wishlist saved a page at a
  time comes together as one. Save each Gumroad wishlist's or Jinxxy list's own page, not the page listing them.
- Each time a wishlist is read again, it holds what the store's list does now, and a product whose price changed
  shows the new price with the old one struck through; one that's cheaper than before is marked (an arrow down,
  in gold), and one that costs more has an arrow up, and **Tasks** lists the cheaper ones. A name, note or picture you gave a wishlist stays.
- **Read it again** and **Quick refresh** (5.2), on a wishlist read from a store, read that store's lists again:
  **Read it again** all of them, and **Quick refresh** only the start of this one (its first 2 pages, or its first
  50 products, whichever is fewer), keeping what it doesn't reach, and leaving the store's other lists as they are.
  A list that isn't on the store any more says so.
- A product a Jinxxy list sends you to on a site that isn't one of the stores (the creator's own, say) shows **Buy
  on** that site, and opens its page there (from 5.1.2). It stays in Hoard: a shared set leaves that link out, as its
  page opens only the stores' own.
- **New wishlist** makes one of your own, which you add products to as you would to a set, or with **Add products by
  link...**.

An open wishlist (from 5.1) shows its products in two groups: **Not yours yet**, with what each costs, and **Yours
already**. Click a group's heading to fold it away; it stays folded next time. From 5.1.1 they're in the order
**Sort** says: **Name**, **Creator** (those with none last) or **Store**; **Recently added** keeps the wishlist's own
order, as the store lists it.

The filters on the left are the wishlist's own (from 5.1.1; before, its tags were above it):

- **Suggested**: words several of its products' names share, with how many have each. Click some to show only the
  products whose names have them all.
- **Your tags**: the tags you gave those of its products that are in your library.
- **Creators**: who made its products, with how many each. Click one to show only theirs.

They start again when you open another wishlist, or leave it. Above the groups:

- **Price**: a range, from and to, in one of the wishlist's currencies (the one most of its prices are in, to begin
  with; choose another beside it). A product priced in another currency, or with no price, is left out while a range
  is set. **Clear** clears the range.

The search box at the top narrows it down too.

Hoard for Unity's **Sets** pill doesn't list wishlists: they're what you want, not what you use together.

## Where sets are kept

Sets and wishlists are in `sets.json` in Hoard's app-data folder (see [Where Hoard keeps things](Where-Hoard-Keeps-Things)), and in
backups (**Settings**, **Backup**). A backup made while your hidden library is locked leaves hidden products out
of every set.
